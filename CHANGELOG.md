# Changelog

## Widget, fixed terms worth it, cleaner pictures

- A Sol widget for your home screen shows your income an hour. Stretch it
  from 1 to 4 cells wide. It updates when you leave the game and every 30
  minutes, and tapping it opens Sol.
- Fixed terms pay far more: 20% for 1 day, 66% for 3 days, 170% for 7
  days, so locking money beats dividends. Not while a loan is running.
- Picture fixes everywhere: no stray bars across the sun, companies stand
  on the street, a tidier home room, a smaller taxi, rounded casino banner.
- Casino tidy-up: roulette, chip bar, blackjack shoe, Plinko edges, wheel
  labels, and confetti stays inside the tower card.
- Claim rows show the amount under the name, with Claim on the right.
- The "tired, rest a bit" note is gone from Earn (taps still tire after a
  long non-stop run).

## Seasons, medals, Tower and a sharper casino

- **Weekly season** on Goals: goals, suns and your first tap each day fill
  a bar. Five rewards a week: cash, a Rush and a Frenzy. Nothing paid.
- **Medals**: achievements are shown as medals in groups.
- **Days played** on Profile and Records. It never resets. Days 7, 14, 30,
  60, 100 and 200 pay about an hour of income.
- **Profile**: a rank title and a showcase of four things you're proud of.
- **Company upgrades**: five named steps per company, +6% income each,
  opening at stages 2, 4, 6, 8 and 10. The company page shows the next one.
- **Fixed terms** in the Bank: lock cash for 1, 3 or 7 days. Break early
  and get 95% back.
- **Tower**, a new casino game: climb row by row and cash out any time.
- Bet bar has ½ and ×2. Each game shows your last five results. Crash can
  skip to the result.
- Claimed money flies to your cash.
- More detail in every picture: casino tables, slots, Sun Forge, scenes,
  the tower, items, miners and your home.
- Fixes from a money stress test: a long freeze or coming back to the app
  can't pay twice; bad numbers can't break a bet, loan or trade; a restored
  backup gets fresh jackpot luck. Many
  play-test exploits closed too.

## Casino jackpot

- A shared jackpot in every casino game except blackjack and video poker
  (they already pay back about 99%). It is shown in the casino and on each
  game. It is super rare (about 1 in 12,000 bets). It starts at a day of
  your income, grows with every bet and stops at 3 days. A max bet wins all
  of it; a smaller bet wins its share.
- The pot is counted in hours of income, so it keeps pace as your empire
  grows.
- To fund it, wins in those games pay 98% of the table. Cash out buttons
  show what you really get, and each game's info sheet says so. The return
  over time stays about the same.
- A jackpot gets a gold celebration that never blocks taps, even in a losing
  Plinko bucket. Records keep your biggest jackpot.
- Plinko's bucket numbers are bigger and easier to read sideways. Crash's
  "Climbing" label sits on a dark backing so the curve can't hide it.

## More money, combos that pay, miner upgrades

- Tapping in a row pays more: from a 10 combo each tap is worth more, up to
  x2 at 100 in a row (shown next to the combo), then it stops climbing.
  The auto clicker doesn't get it.
- Companies earn 6% more. The game now takes about a month to finish
  instead of five weeks.
- Miner upgrades (Invest > Crypto): Faster chips (+15% mining a level) and
  Better cooling (lets mining pay more of your company income).

## Landscape

- Turn your phone: every screen works sideways as well as upright. The tab
  bar becomes a rail on the left with Back under it, pages sit in a centred
  column, Earn puts your money and buttons beside the tower, casino games
  put the table on the left and the controls on the right, and pop-ups use
  two columns so their button is always in view.
- Turning the phone keeps whatever is open: pages, pop-ups, typed amounts
  and casino rounds.
- Settings > Auto-rotate (on). Turn it off to keep the game upright.

- Records (Profile): richest you've been, best income, best day, best

- Rivals: 30 tycoons with real-looking fortunes shown to the pound

- Rebirth tree: 229 nodes (was 157), a deep path and a new keystone on every

  behind them any more.
- Items say what they do: your bonus now, what each full set adds in £ an

- Expansions cost far less from stage 3 on (prices grow x2.6 a stage, was
  x3.3; a stage 9 Shop expansion was 2,300 hours of its income, now 500), and

  45% more so the game still takes weeks.
- Mining pays at most a quarter of what your companies earn.
- A rebirth after months of waiting can no longer buy the whole tree at once.
- Fixed: a home you buy shows under Yours straight away. Tree nodes that
  make deals come sooner now work.

## Better Level up

- Each level now nearly doubles your tap's base value (x1.9, was x1.64)
  and costs x3.1 more than the last (was x3.4). Level 4 to 5: about £30K
  for +£16 a tap (was £39K for +£7). Your level is kept; taps just get

- Robot: 90% done on day 35 to 37 (was 39), still inside the target.

## Fast tapping and tapping apps

- Taps past the 7-a-second limit no longer show "+£0"; they just light the

  tapping app, say), taps pay a quarter until you rest a little. Normal
  play never hits it. Earn shows "tired, rest a bit" when it happens.
- Tap sound and buzz play at most about 14 times a second, so very fast
  tapping stays smooth.

## Sell companies, manager switch, centred pop-ups, less text
